Welcome to FinAgent¶

FinAgent is a local-first personal finance assistant for the bank statements you already download. It files your statements, categorizes transactions with rules and optional lookup/model tools, and turns the results into a private dashboard on your own machine.

No bank login is required. Your statement files, settings, rules, and processed results stay in folders you control.

Start here¶

  • Installing for the first time? Open Installation in the navigation and choose your platform, or use Quick Start.
  • Ready to add data? Import explains supported statement files, account labels, dates, and where each file goes.
  • Want to understand a run? Process walks through every stage and the controls that protect existing categories.
  • Reviewing or splitting purchases? Transactions explains category edits, split allocation, and analytics behavior.
  • Planning recurring costs or goals? Budget covers budget sections, Wishlist / Piggy Bank goals, and planned progress.
  • Choosing lookup or AI options? Begin with Settings, then use the focused Location Engine and AI Engine guides.

The everyday flow¶

  1. Configure FinAgent in Settings.
  2. Add one or more bank statements in Import.
  3. Start the workflow from Process.
  4. Explore Overview, Monthly, Transactions, and Budget.
  5. Correct a transaction or add a Rule when a merchant needs a better match.

The How to Use section in the navigation contains a page for each step and feature. For profiles, command-line runs, and maintenance tools, open Advanced Use.

Local by default

Ollama runs categorization on your machine. Optional map services and cloud models contact their named providers only when you select them; their pages explain exactly what is sent before you enable one.
